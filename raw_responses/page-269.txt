Concerning this variety of the Olympic games due to the intercalation of a month, take it thus: the ordinary *Tetraeteris* is of 1447 days, the embolimic of 1477 days; of which sort the first of this period is, on account of the intercalation of *Posideon*: which makes fifty months in all, while the ordinary one is filled out in only forty-nine months.
